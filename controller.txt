Required Components
Arduino Board (e.g., Arduino UNO or Nano)

2WD or 4WD Robot Car Chassis (includes TT gear motors and wheels)

Motor Driver Module (L298N or WitPro Motor Driver Block)

Wireless Module (HC-05 Bluetooth, IR receiver, or Wi-Fi block)

Battery Power Source (e.g., 2x 18650 Li-ion batteries or a 9V battery pack)

Jumper Wires (Male-to-Male and Male-to-Female)
